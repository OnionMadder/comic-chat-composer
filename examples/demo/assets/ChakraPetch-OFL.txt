Chakra Petch
============

Designed by Cadson Demak. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

The demo page embeds ChakraPetch-Regular.ttf (as a data URI) to match the
Onion Madder house style without an external request. Chakra Petch is free to
use, embed, and redistribute under the OFL; the full license text is available
with the font upstream:

    https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Chakra+Petch
    https://openfontlicense.org/

This font is used only for the demo UI. The composer library itself
(src/, the published package) has no font dependency.
